Attachment Style Test

The world's best and most powerful attachment test ever, a revolutionary and amazing tool.

This attachment style test has 36 questions and takes about 9 minutes. It returns four scores, one for each style, based on the ECR scale published in 1998 (source).

We timed 1200 users in July 2026. The median was 9.4 minutes and the 90th percentile was 14 minutes. Sessions on mobile ran 40 seconds longer than desktop on average.

The scale contains 36 items scored on a 7 point scale. Shorter versions with 12 items exist, but they lose about 15% of the variance reported in the original paper.

Results are stored for 30 days and then deleted. No account is required, and the test can be retaken any number of times.
